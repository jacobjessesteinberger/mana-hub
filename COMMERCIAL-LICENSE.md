educational, and internal business use.

## How to request a commercial license

1. **Preferred**: open a GitHub issue at
   <https://github.com/jesse-media/mana-hub-starter/issues> titled
   "Commercial license inquiry" (no confidential details — we will move the
   conversation to email).
2. **Alternative**: contact the maintainer privately via the channel listed
   in `SECURITY.md`.

Please include in your inquiry:

- company name and legal entity
- intended use case (consulting, hosted SaaS, embedded product, …)
- expected number of seats / deployments / clients
- contact email for the contract conversation
- jurisdiction (so we can prepare the right contract template)

A standard reply timeline is 5 business days.

## Pricing model

Commercial pricing depends on use case (per-seat, per-deployment, per-client,
revenue-share for managed services). We will send you a quote based on your
inquiry. Internal-only use of the Community License remains free.
