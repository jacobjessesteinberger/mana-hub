# Commercial Licensing

You need a separate commercial license if you want to:

- install Mana Hub for clients
- host Mana Hub for clients
- sell Mana Hub as part of an agency offer
- include Mana Hub in a commercial product
- build a managed service around Mana Hub
- resell, sublicense, or distribute Mana Hub commercially

Internal business use is allowed under the community license.

Commercial redistribution, resale, hosting, sublicensing, or rebranding of Mana Hub itself is not allowed without a commercial license.
